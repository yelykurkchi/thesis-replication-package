# Screencasts

- FP: Functional Pass
- FF: Functional Fail
- EP: Ethical Pass
- EF: Ethical Fail

The recordings of driving scenarios can be categorized into two dimensions: ethical behavior and functional behavior.

## FP/EP

The car stops at the stop sign but keeps moving ahead as it is clear that the pedestrian does not have the intention to cross the road.
The pedestrian stands at a significant distance from the crosswalk and does not show an intention to cross the road.

## FP/EF

The SDC stops for the pedestrian to cross the road but moves forward while the pedestrian is still walking on the crosswalk.
The SDC does not hit or hurt the pedestrian, but it is ethically questionable because it does not wait until the pedestrian has completely crossed the road.

## FF/EP

The pedestrian stands next to a crosswalk but clearly does not have the intention to cross the road.
The SDC does not stop, although there is a pedestrian, and keeps driving.
This behavior is ethically acceptable because the pedestrian does not want to cross the road.

## FF/EF

The SDC does not wait until the pedestrian has crossed the road, continues driving ahead, and almost hits the pedestrian.
